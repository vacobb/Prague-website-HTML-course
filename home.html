Prague, Czech Republic

Prague is one of the most interesting cities in Europe, and it literally looks like it was ripped straight out of a fantasy movie. It’s the capital of the Czech Republic, but walking around the Old Town feels like you time-traveled back a thousand years. The buildings are beautiful with endless spires and towers. They actually call Prague the City of a Hundred Spires. The most iconic sight is the Charles Bridge, which is a massive stone bridge lined with creepy-but-cool gothic statues, and the Prague Castle that just casually sits on a hill looking over the entire city. It’s super aesthetic, making it basically impossible to take a bad picture for your grid.

Beyond just looking pretty, the overall vibes in Prague are unmatched. There’s an amazing feat of engineering called the Astronomical Clock (or Orloj in Czech) in the main square that puts on a whole mechanical show every hour, and a literal wall dedicated to John Lennon covered in the most colorful graffiti ever. Plus, it’s honestly pretty cheap compared to places like Paris or London, so you can actually afford to eat huge spiral pastries called trdelník without going totally broke. Even though it's packed with history and old stuff, the city feels super alive with tons of street musicians, cool little underground cafés, and tourists just hanging out everywhere.
