Phrase Book

Czech has a very rich phonological landscape. Ranging from its twelve vowels to its infamous consonant clusters, Czech words can feel a bit intimidating at first. To familiarize yourself with the sounds of the language and learn to pronounce the words you are likely to come across, check out this video.

Once you feel more comfortable with the sounds of Czech, try saying the vocab below out loud or check out the attractions page to practice your pronunciation.

Small Talk
Dobrý den! Hello!
Jak se máš? How are you?
Mám se dobře. I am well.
Na shledanou! Good-bye!
Prosím. Please.
Děkuji. Thank you.
Mluvíte anglicky? Do you speak English?
Nemluvím česky. I don't speak Czech.
Můžete mi pomoct? Could you help me?
ano / jo yes
ne no

Food
Dobrý den! Hello!
Co si dáš k jídlo / pití? What would you like to eat / drink?
Dám si... I would like...
colu a cola
vodu a water
pivo a beer
burger a hranolky a burger and French fries
knedlíky dumplings
Zaplatím. I would like to pay.
Zvlášť nebo dohromady? Separate or together?
Kartou nebo hotově? Card or cash?
