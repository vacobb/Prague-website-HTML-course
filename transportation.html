Tramvaj

(Tram)

One of the best ways to see the city is to take a picturesque ride through town on a tram. With an extensive network across the entire city, odds are that if you can think of a place, the tram can take you there. Trams come by often during the day, but after 10:00pm, they switch to night hours and come by once every 20-30 minutes. So, plan accordingly if you are planning to tram home after a night of dancing and drinking.

×

Metro (Metro)

The metro is great for getting around quickly. The metro network is not as extensive as the aboveground tram network, but it gets you where you need to go and it does it quickly. Trains come every 3 minutes during the workday, and every 6 minutes on nights and weekends.

×

Vlak

(Train)

Of some use within city limits, the train is much more suited for traveling in and out of Prague. If you want to take a trip anywhere in the country, the train is a great option. It is quick, on-time, and stops in many large and small cities.

Prague has a vast network of public transportation. It is one of the most well-connected cities I have ever been to. Purchase your ticket on the PiD app and then you have unlimited use of the trams, metro, trains (within city limits), and even bike-share bicicles.
