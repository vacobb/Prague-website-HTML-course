Old Town Square

(Staroměstské náměstí)

This square is home to a variety of architechtural styles. It is the heart of the city, centrally located and well connected to everything you could need.

×

Astronomical Clock

(Orloj)

Built in 1490 by Jan Růže, this medieval astronomical clock is attached to the Old Town Hall in Prague. It is the centerpiece of the Old Town Square.

×

Prague Castle

(Pražský hrad)

At about 70,000 square meters, this is the largest castle complex in the world and the home of Czechia's president. This castle sits high on a hill overlooking the vast city of Prague.

×

Petřín

From the tower at the top of the hill you will have access to some of the most scenic views of the city and Vltava River below.

×

Žižkov Television Tower

(Žižkovský vysílač)

One of the highest points in the city, the views here are unmatched. And if sights of the city aren't your thing, many people come to see the babies crawling all over this tower.
